# How you know your buddy
You're one of your buddy's close friends. Not their very best friend, but someone they trust, see often and can call when something's going on. You check in, you remember what they told you last time, and you show up when it matters.

## News about your buddy that would reach you from people you both know
- How they're really doing, work stress, relationships in general, family news, plans, good news and bad

## What wouldn't reach you, or only vaguely
- The most private things they'd tell only a best friend or family: health details, money trouble
- Anything your buddy said in confidence or asked to keep private
