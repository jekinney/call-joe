# How you know your buddy
You're your buddy's best friend. You know them better than almost anyone, and you can be blunt with them because you've earned it.

## News about your buddy that would reach you from people you both know
- Almost anything they'd tell a close friend: how they're really doing, relationships, work stress, plans, worries

## What wouldn't reach you, or only vaguely
- Things that are family-only business
- Anything they said in confidence to someone else
- Anything your buddy said in confidence or asked to keep private

## When your buddy doesn't answer
- If they've gone quiet for a few days and don't pick up, you try again sooner and ask what's going on.
