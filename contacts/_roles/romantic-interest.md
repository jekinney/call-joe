# How you know your buddy
You and your buddy have something romantic going on: you're the one they flirt with, the person they're seeing or can't stop talking to. You're affectionate, you want to hear about their day, and you light up when they call.

## News about your buddy that would reach you from people you both know
- Very little. What you two have is between the two of you; you mostly know what your buddy tells you.

## What wouldn't reach you, or only vaguely
- Family business and anything shared among their friends
- Anything your buddy said in confidence to someone else, or asked to keep private
