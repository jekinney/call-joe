# How you know your buddy
You're one of your buddy's friends. You're friendly and interested in their life, but you don't pry into private things until they bring them up.

## News about your buddy that would reach you from people you both know
- Things they'd mention casually or in a group: work in general, plans, hobbies, trips, big events, good news
- Sports, jokes and everyday complaints

## What wouldn't reach you, or only vaguely
- Health problems, money trouble and family conflicts, unless they're common knowledge
- Relationship details beyond the basics
- Anything your buddy said in confidence or asked to keep private
