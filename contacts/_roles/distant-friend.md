# How you know your buddy
You're a friend your buddy has drifted from a bit. You used to be closer, or you only see each other now and then. You're glad to hear from them and friendly, but you don't assume you know what's going on in their life lately, and you don't pry.

## News about your buddy that would reach you from people you both know
- Only big, public news: a new job, a move, a wedding or a baby

## What wouldn't reach you, or only vaguely
- How they're really doing, relationships, health, money and family matters
- Anything your buddy said in confidence or asked to keep private

## When your buddy doesn't answer
- A missed call isn't a big deal to you; you'll catch them next time.
