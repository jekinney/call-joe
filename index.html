Your API key

Your friends think with Claude, using your own Anthropic account. You pay Anthropic directly for what you use. To get a key:

  1. Go to console.anthropic.com and sign up or sign in.
  2. Under Billing, add a little credit.
  3. Open API keys, click Create key, name it "Call Joe", and copy it.
  4. Paste it below. It's saved only on this computer.

No key yet? Skip it and add it later in Settings → Account. You can't call anyone until you do.

Add contact

For now, Claude builds new friends with you. Open Claude Code in the Call Joe folder and type:

/new-contact

Have ready: their name and a few sentences about who they are, a 10 to 20 second voice clip (someone who's okay with it), and a made-up face picture for video calls. Claude writes their personality, shows it to you, and sets everything up.

Or put a ready-made friend on your phone: Settings → Friends.

Tips

  • Tap a friend, then 📞 to call or 🎥 for video.
  • Talk over them to interrupt, or press the spacebar or ✋.
  • They hang up on their own when you've both said bye.
  • Miss a call and they leave a voicemail: Calls → Voicemail.
  • Turn friends on or off: Contacts → a friend → Edit.
  • Change the wallpaper, phone outline and call screen: Settings → Looks.
  • The buttons at the very bottom work like Android: ◁ back, ○ home, □ recent calls. Esc goes back too.

Clear recent calls?

Get a Google AI key

  1. Open Google AI Studio: aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Click Create API key and pick (or create) a project.
  3. Picture-making needs billing turned on: click Set up billing next to the key and add a card in Google Cloud.
  4. Copy the key (it starts with AIza), paste it in Google AI key here, and click Save.

Only new photos use it, about 4 cents each; pictures already in a friend's album are free. The photo description and reference pictures from the album are sent to Google. The key is saved only on this computer and is never deleted, not even by Reset phone. In airplane mode, or without a key, photos are made on your graphics card instead.

Get a free GIPHY key

  1. Open the GIPHY developer site: developers.giphy.com/dashboard
  2. Click Sign Up (or Log In if you have a GIPHY account) and confirm your email.
  3. On the dashboard, click Create an API Key.
  4. Choose API (not SDK), then Next Step.
  5. Name the app Call Joe, add a short description like "Personal texting app", agree to the terms, and click Create App.
  6. Your key appears under the app name. It's a long string of letters and numbers. Click it to copy.
  7. Come back here, paste it in GIPHY key, and click Save.

The free key is a "beta" key with a limit of about 100 searches an hour, plenty for personal texting. The key is saved only on this computer and is never deleted, not even by Reset phone.
