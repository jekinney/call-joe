Add a friend

For now, Claude builds new friends with you. Open Claude Code in the Call Joe folder and type:

/new-contact

Have ready: their name and a few sentences about who they are, a 10 to 20 second voice clip (someone who's okay with it), and a made-up face picture for video calls. Claude writes their personality, shows it to you, and sets everything up.

Tips

  • Tap a friend, then 📞 to call or 🎥 for video.
  • Talk over them to interrupt, or press the spacebar or ✋.
  • They hang up on their own when you've both said bye.
  • Miss a call and they leave a voicemail: Calls → Voicemail.
  • Turn friends on or off: Contacts → a friend → Edit.
  • Change the wallpaper, phone outline and call screen: Settings → Looks.
  • The buttons at the very bottom work like Android: ◁ back, ○ home, □ recent calls. Esc goes back too.
